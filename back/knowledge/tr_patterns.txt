Global URI Predicate for a Class
Let C be a class in the target ontology and let n scheme of the source relational schema S, whose tuples generate instances of C.
The predicate URI_R​(r,s) holds if and only if s is the URI generated for the tuple r ∈ R.
The URI for the tuple r∈R is constructed by concatenating:
   - a namespace prefix P​ associated with the class C; and
   - the values of the attributes in the primary key of R.
Formally,
URI_R(r,s):= hasURI(P_C, PK(r), s)  where:
   - P_C​ is the namespace prefix associated with class C;
   - PK(r)  is the value of attributes forming the primary key of tuple r;
   - s is the generated URI.
The predicate hasURI must guarantee that distinct tuples of R generate distinct URIs.

Relational Path:
Let S=(R,Ω) be a relational schema.
A relational path φ from relation scheme R1 to relation scheme Rn
is a finite sequence:
   φ = [F1, ..., Fn−1]
such that, for each i ∈ {1,...,n−1}, Fi is a foreign key relating Ri and Ri+1.
φ(r,g) holds iff there exists a sequence of tuples r1,...,rn such that:
   - r1 = r
   - rn = g
   - each pair (ri,ri+1) is connected by Fi
The operator Relations(·) applied to φ
returns the set of all relation schemes occurring along the path. 
Formally, 
   Relations(φ) = {R1, R2, . . . , Rn}. 

TRANSFORMATION RULES PATTERNS 
Class Transformation Rule (CTR)
A Class Transformation Rule associated with class C has the form:
   ψ: C(s)←R(r), URI_R​(r,s), δ(r)
where:
   - ψ is the identifier of the transformation rule;
   - C is a class; 
   - R is a relation scheme or virtual table in the source schema S;
   - r is a tuple variable ranging over tuples of R;
   - s is the URI generated for tuple r;
   - URI_R​(r,s) generates the URI of the RDF resource corresponding to r;
   - δ(r) is an optional selection predicate over tuples of R.
The relation R is called the pivot relation of the rule, and tuples of R are called pivot tuples. 
Distinct pivot tuples must generate distinct RDF resources.
Example: foaf:Organization(s) ← empresa(e), URI_empresa(e, s)

Object Type Transformation Rule (OTR)
   ψ: P(s, o) ← RD(d), BD[d,s], RG(g), BG[g, o], φ(d, g)
where:
   - ψ is the identifier of the OTR.
   - P is an object property in V with domain D and range G. 
   - “RD(d), BD[d,s]” is the RHS of the CTR ψD that matches class D with pivot relation RD of ψD.
   - “RG(g), BG[g, o]” is the RHS of the CTR ψG that matches class G with pivot relation RG ψG.
   - φ  is a path from RD to relation RG  
   - φ(d, g) holds iff d is connected to g following the path ϕ 

Data Type Transformation Rule (DTR)
Local-DTR (the attributes are from the pivot relation) 
ψ: P(s, v) ← RD(r), BD[r, s], nonNull(g.A1), . . . ,nonNull(g.Ak), RDFLiteral(g.A1, "A1", RD, v1), . . . , RDFLiteral(g.Ak, "Ak", RD, vk), T([v1, . . . , vk], v),
where
   - ψ is the identifier of the DTR.
   - P is a datatype property in V with domain D. 
   - “RD(r), BD[r, x]” is the right-hand side for the CTR that matches class D with pivot relation RD. 
   - A1, . . . , Ak are attributes of RD. 
   - T is an optional function that transforms values of attributes A1, . . . , Ak to values of property P.

Path-DTR (the attributes are from a related relation) 
ψ: P(s, v) ← RD(r), BD[r, s], RG(g), nonNull(g.A1), . . . ,nonNull(g. Ak), RDFLiteral(g.A1, "A1", RG, v1), . . . , RDFLiteral(g.Ak, "Ak", RG, vk), T([v1, . . . , vk], v), φ(r, g)
 where
- ψ is the identifier of the DTR.
- P is a datatype property in V with domain D. 
- “RD(r), BD[s, x]” is the right-hand side for the CTR that matches class D with pivot relation RD. 
- φ  is an optional path from RD to relation RG.  
- A1, . . . , Ak are the attributes of RG.  
- T is an optional function that transforms values of attributes A1, . . . , Ak to values of property P.
