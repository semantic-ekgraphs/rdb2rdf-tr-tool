**Validation notes**

- The pivots are `manufacturers` and `products`. Their declared primary keys (`id` and `product_id`) provide the required identity attributes; `sku` is unique but is not the product pivot key.
- The key-based subject templates are injective within each pivot when instantiated with canonical integer values. The shared, undelimited template can nevertheless cause cross-pivot IRI collisions and should be replaced with relation-specific templates.
- The `brand` mapping is a regular OTR: it follows a functional many-to-one foreign-key path from `products` to the referenced `manufacturers` row. The FK has no explicit name in the supplied metadata, so the path above describes it by its columns rather than claiming a constraint name.
- Overall, the mappings satisfy the entity-preserving patterns based on the supplied keys and foreign key, subject to correcting the URI-template risks and confirming the intended ontology treatment of `homepage`.
