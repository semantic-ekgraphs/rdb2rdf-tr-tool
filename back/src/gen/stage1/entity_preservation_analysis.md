# Entity-Preservation Analisys

**Assessment basis.** The report treats the relation supplying the subject identity as the pivot relation. A mapping is fully verifiable as entity-preserving only when the supplied schema establishes that its URI constructor is injective over pivot tuples—for example, by declaring the placeholder attribute as a primary key or unique key. The metadata does not provide those key constraints for `artist.gid`, `medium.id`, or `track.id`, so the identity-preservation conclusions for those pivots are conditional. An omitted constraint is not proof that the value is non-unique.

**Overall result.** The supplied mappings largely have entity-preserving CTR/DTR shapes: their subjects are constructed from apparent pivot relations, and their literal properties are sourced either from those pivots or from related rows. However, the supplied metadata does **not** establish uniqueness of the subject-key columns `artist.gid`, `medium.id`, and `track.id`, so distinct-tuple identity preservation cannot be proven from the extract alone. The `owl:sameAs` mapping is the principal structural exception: its transformed external IRI is not backed by a target pivot and CTR, so it is not demonstrated to satisfy the specification’s pivot-to-pivot OTR pattern. The annotation and DBpedia relational paths also need complete schema-level FK validation.
