### Preservation note

The manufacturer and product CTRs preserve row identity through their respective primary-key-based subject templates. The local DTRs attach values from attributes of those same pivot relations. The `schema:brand` rule is a schema-grounded OTR through the declared foreign key. The `schema:url` mapping is retained above as an **OTR-shaped, nonconforming case** so the supplied R2RML behavior is represented without asserting a relational entity relationship that the schema does not establish. To make the mapping conform to the stated entity-preserving framework, map `homepage` as a datatype value, or replace it with an FK-backed relationship to a represented entity.
