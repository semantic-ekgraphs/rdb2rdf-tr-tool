# Compiled Transformation Rules

**Notation.** `URI_manufacturers(r,s)` and `URI_products(r,s)` are the URI constructors induced by the supplied subject templates:

- `URI_manufacturers(r,s) ≡ hasURI("http://example.com⟨h⟩", ⟨r.id⟩, s)`

The templates are preserved exactly as supplied. Although each primary-key template is injective within its own pivot relation, the shared, undelimited template may produce cross-relation IRI collisions; relation-specific, delimited templates are recommended. There are no selection conditions or SQL transformation functions.
